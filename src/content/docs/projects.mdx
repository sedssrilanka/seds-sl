# Managing Projects

Projects represent the various events, research initiatives, and missions undertaken by SEDS Sri Lanka and its chapters.

## 1. Accessing Projects in Keystatic

1. Navigate to `/keystatic` in your browser.
2. Under the **Collections** section, select **Projects & Flagships**.
3. You will see all current projects (e.g., Asteroid Search Campaign, Observe the Moon Night).

## 2. Creating a Project

1. Click **"Create"** in the top right corner.
2. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Project Name:** The official title of the project or flagship.
   - **Slug:** Automatically generated from the title for URL routing (e.g. `/projects/asteroid-search`).
   - **Short Description:** A concise summary displayed on project cards and previews.
   - **Thumbnail / Cover Image:** Upload a high-resolution banner image.
   - **Chapter Name (Optional):** Specify the owning university chapter (e.g. SEDS Mora, SEDS UoP), or leave empty for national projects.
   - **Feature this Project:** Toggle on to highlight the project on the homepage spotlight.
   - **Custom Page Link (Optional):** Provide a dedicated route (e.g. `/projects/observe-the-moon-night`) if custom interactive components are used.
   - **Project Details & Content:** Use the Markdoc rich-text editor for markdown text, headings, lists, and embedded media.

## 3. Saving & Publishing

Click **"Save"** in the top right. In production, Keystatic directly commits your Markdoc files to the GitHub repository, which automatically triggers a fresh deployment.
