# Administering the Merchandise Shop

The SEDS platform features a modern e-commerce storefront with product catalog managed by Keystatic CMS and zero-database checkout powered by Tally Forms and email automation.

![Shop Admin Panel](https://placehold.co/800x400/0f172a/ffffff?text=Keystatic+CMS:+Products+and+Orders)

## 1. Creating Products

1. Navigate to `/keystatic` and click **Products & Merchandise**.
2. Click **Create Product**.

### Pricing

Prices are configured in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). Specify the exact amount (e.g. `3500` for LKR 3,500).

### Pre-Orders & Stock Status

Products support in-stock badges and pre-order modes:
- **In Stock**: Enables immediate ordering.
- **Pre-Order Item**: Automatically displays "Pre-Order Now" badges on the storefront and product detail pages.

## 2. Order Management

Orders are submitted via integrated Tally forms with instant bank transfer receipts:
- Real-time webhook notifications route order alerts to administrators via Resend emails.
- Interactive email studio is available at `/admin/emails` for live previewing of order confirmations, alerts, and shipping updates.
