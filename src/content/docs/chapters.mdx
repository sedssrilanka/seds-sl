# Managing Chapters

SEDS Chapters represent the individual university or institutional branches of SEDS Sri Lanka. This guide shows how to add, edit, and manage these branches.

![Chapters Admin Panel](https://placehold.co/800x400/0f172a/ffffff?text=Keystatic+CMS:+Chapters)

## 1. Navigating to the Chapters Collection

1. Access Keystatic CMS by navigating to `/keystatic`.
2. Under the **Collections** section, click on **Chapters**.
3. You will see a list of all existing university and junior chapters.

## 2. Creating a New Chapter

1. Click the **"Create"** button in the top right corner.
2. **Basic Information:**
   - **Name**: The full name of the chapter (e.g., "SEDS UOC").
   - **Slug**: Generates automatically from the name, used in the URL (e.g., `/chapters/seds-uoc`).
   - **Main Image**: Upload a high-quality featured image representing the university or chapter.
   - **Description**: A short summary that appears on chapter cards and directory listings.
3. **Contact & Social Links:**
   - Input the chapter's official contact email.
   - Add social media profiles (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.).
4. **Rich Content:**
   - The **Content** editor uses structured Markdoc, allowing markdown text, headings, lists, and images to describe the chapter's projects and history in detail.
