# SECTION 3: Live Explanatory Briefing Scripts
*(Ready-to-read 30–60 second explanatory scripts for SEDS Sri Lanka commentators as SEDS Celestia's telescope feed locks onto each target)*

```
[TARGET: PLATO CRATER — FEED BY SEDS CELESTIA]
"On your screen right now, through the SEDS Celestia telescope feed, is Plato Crater. 
Spanning 109 kilometers on the northern boundary of Mare Imbrium, Plato is famous for its 
remarkably smooth, dark interior. This darkness is due to ancient basaltic lava that 
welled up and filled the floor. Notice the sharp, needle-like shadows cast by its jagged rim peaks."

[TARGET: ALPINE VALLEY / VALLIS ALPES — FEED BY SEDS CELESTIA]
"We are now zooming into the Alpine Valley—Vallis Alpes. SEDS Celestia is giving us a clear view 
of this 166-kilometer-long lunar canyon cutting cleanly through the Montes Alpes. It is a classic 
graben structure, formed when the lunar crust pulled apart along parallel faults and dropped down."

[TARGET: APENNINE MOUNTAINS & APOLLO 15 — FEED BY SEDS CELESTIA]
"Look at the dramatic peaks of Montes Apenninus rising over 5,000 meters into space! Right at the 
base of these mountains lies the landing site of Apollo 15 and the sinuous canyon known as 
Hadley Rille. In 1971, astronauts drove the Lunar Roving Vehicle right along the edge of that channel."

[TARGET: THE STRAIGHT WALL / RUPES RECTA — FEED BY SEDS CELESTIA]
"SEDS Celestia is now centered on the Straight Wall—Rupes Recta—in Mare Nubium. This is a 
110-kilometer-long normal fault line where one side of the crust dropped by nearly 300 meters. 
Because the Sun is low on the horizon, it casts an intense, pencil-thin black shadow across the mare."

[TARGET: ALPHONSUS CRATER & CATENA DAVY — FEED BY SEDS CELESTIA]
"Notice the prominent central peak inside Alphonsus Crater. If you observe the outer edges of the 
crater floor, you can see dark patches—these are pyroclastic volcanic vents that blasted dark ash 
billions of years ago. Just nearby is Catena Davy, a string of more than 20 micro-craters from a fragmented comet."

[TARGET: MARE TRANQUILLITATIS & APOLLO 11 — FEED BY SEDS CELESTIA]
"Panning east across Mare Tranquillitatis—the Sea of Tranquility. This ancient plain of solidified 
basalt is rich in titanium. On July 20, 1969, Apollo 11 touched down here at Statio Tranquillitatis, 
where Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin made humanity's very first footprints on the lunar surface."
```
